Honest answers

Do customers need to install an app?

No. They scan the QR and the ticket opens in their browser. No sign-up and no phone number.

What if a customer has no phone?

Their number is still yours to call out loud. Many shops keep a few paper tickets or discs for them.

Do alerts work on iPhone?

Yes, while the page is open. For alerts on a locked iPhone, the customer adds the ticket to their Home Screen first; the page shows how.

What if our internet goes down?

Staff need a connection to notify. Customers can still see their number, so calling it out works as a backup.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Free plan has no time limit and needs no card. Pro (₱299 a month) and Max (₱999 a month) add counters, staff, a display screen, branding and reports.

What do you store?

Your name and email, and your queue names. For customers, only the optional first name, cleared every night.
