Privacy notice

Aqona helps shops tell customers when it's their turn. We collect as little as we can. This notice explains what we keep, why, and how to delete it.

Customers in a queue

  • Customers never sign up and never give a phone number or email.
  • A first name is optional, so staff can call it out.
  • If the customer allows alerts, we keep their browser's push address so we can notify that phone.
  • All of this is deleted when the queue starts a new day (every night by default), or earlier if the shop removes the ticket.

Shop owners and staff

  • Your name and email address, used to log you in and show who you are.
  • If you sign in with Google, the link between your Google account and Aqona (we never see your Google password).
  • Your sessions (device and network address), to keep you logged in and to stop abuse.
  • Your workspace and queue names, and each queue's settings.

Who processes this data

  • Cloudflare hosts Aqona and stores its data.
  • Google, only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Better Auth Infrastructure may receive log-in events (such as time, device and network address) to protect accounts from abuse and to help us look after the service.

We do not sell data, show customers' data to other shops, or send marketing messages.

Deleting your data

Owners can delete a queue, or their whole account, from their dashboard. Deleting your account removes your workspace, every queue in it, and all their tickets straight away.

Questions

This notice follows the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012. For questions or requests about your data, contact the Aqona team.
